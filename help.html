How to use CleanPress

1. Add a recording

Click ＋ Add recordings or drop a file on the window: MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG or a video (MP4, MOV, WEBM). The AI removes the background noise on this computer – the first time it downloads itself once (about 34 MB). A 10-minute recording takes about 1–3 minutes.

2. Listen and adjust

Press ▶ and switch between Original and ✨ Cleaned (or press B) to hear the difference. Click the waveform to jump. Choose what the recording is made for – podcast, YouTube, audiobook (ACX), lecture, voice memo or broadcast – and CleanPress sets the right loudness.

  • Noise removal: 100% removes all background noise; lower keeps a little room sound so the voice sounds natural.
  • Remove rumble: wind, traffic, desk knocks and other deep sounds.
  • Electrical hum (Pro): the 50 or 60 Hz buzz from cables and lights – found automatically.
  • Soften harsh “s” sounds (Pro), Even out loud and quiet speech (Pro), Clearer, warmer voice (Pro) and Shorten long pauses (Pro).

3. Save

Click ⬇ Save cleaned file. CleanPress measures the exact loudness (EBU R128 / ITU BS.1770) and shows a before/after report. For audiobooks, the ACX preset checks RMS, peaks and noise floor.

For a video, CleanPress saves the cleaned sound: replace the audio track in your video editor.
